Buses travel in packs

When two buses of the same route issue tickets at the same stop within 3 minutes, they arrived together. We compare how often that happens with pure chance. 0 = evenly spaced, 1 = as if at random, above 1 = in packs. One bus packed, the next one empty: that's bunching.
